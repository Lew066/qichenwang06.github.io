The Professor

Interactive educational robot

Project
P10
Date
Jul 2025 – Present
Role
Robotics & Embedded Systems Engineer
Status
Active

The Professor is the TSJ Foundation's first interactive educational robot, built for STEM outreach. Qichen joined a project already in progress and redesigned the body plan to add two working arms.

  • 10servos integrated
  • 5DOF per arm
  • 3coordinated motions
  • 50Maestro sequence frames

What I built

  • Installed both arms, including the arm-to-shoulder gear train and servo connections.
  • Built a chest foundation that secures the shoulder servos, gears and chassis to the main body.
  • Integrated 10 servos across the chest and arm assemblies, with 5 degrees of freedom per arm.
  • Programmed 3 motions in Pololu Maestro Control Center by tuning servo position, speed and acceleration across 50 sequence frames.

The three motions

  • Hug. Raises both arms and bends the elbows into a hug.
  • Point the way. Raises the arms, turns them sideways and points in a direction.
  • Wave. Raises an arm, bends the elbow and turns it side to side.

Credits

  • The body shell, head and waist components, and the initial arm CAD, came from the team before me. I reworked their plan so the body could carry arms, then built and programmed them.
  • Pololu Maestro
  • ESP32
  • Servo control
  • Gear & linkage assembly
  • Wiring & power
